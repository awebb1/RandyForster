Magic & Fun For All Occasions

— "Not only is Randy a wonderful magician but he makes you laugh out loud, literally."


Randy Forster A magician with a passion for enchantment; With a perfect mix of mind-bending magic and sharp-witted comedy, Randy transforms every show into an unforgettable experience by summoning wonder and conjuring laughter. From impossible feats to hilarious surprises, he keeps audiences amazed, amused, and always wanting more.

Inspired by a mesmerizing card trick at the Yogi Magic Mart as a young man, he now finds himself mesmerizing others. As a member of Dickens Parlour Theatre in the Bethany Beach, Delaware area, Randy's dazzling performances have delighted guests on countless occasions. From his own shows to community events, private functions, and charity galas, Randy's charismatic style appeals to all ages across the Delmarva region.

Experience the Comedy Magic of Randy Forster – a magician for every occasion!
